How scoring works

Guild score = average of members' season points.

Check-in = 10 base points (one per wallet per UTC day).
× 2 if a SqueekMining round was settled since your last check-in (read on chain).
× 1.25 if your wallet is above the staker threshold at check-in (fixed bonus, not by amount).
Max 25 points per check-in.

Why it holds up: averages punish empty wallets · max 20 seats · one check-in per day · 24 h leave cool-down · 14-day inactivity removal. Many wallets can still be created, but points have no monetary value.

What lives on chain

One small registry contract: guilds, members, check-in days. It never holds SQK, ETH or NFTs. Staked power and rounds are only read from the official Squeekchain contracts.
